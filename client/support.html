Help centre

How can we help?

Find answers to common questions or send the Z team a support request. Financial and identity cases are visible only to the Super Admin; general marketplace questions are handled by the moderator team.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my order?

Sign in and open Orders in your account to review the latest order information. For a delivery delay or an order issue, submit a request under Order & delivery or General buyer dispute.

How do I update a listing or my shop?

Use your seller dashboard to manage product details and storefront information. If you have trouble uploading pictures or editing a listing, choose Listing & storefront help.

Who can help with a payout, fee, or payment problem?

Choose the matching financial topic in the form. These requests are routed to the Super Admin and are not available in the moderator inbox.

Where should I ask about verification or a suspended shop?

Choose the relevant verification or account topic. These requests are private to the Super Admin. Do not include passwords or full payment-card details in a support message.

What should I do if my whole account is suspended?

You cannot access your account while the suspension is active. Use the suspension appeal page and sign in with the suspended account to verify ownership. The page checks for an existing request and tells you if it is still awaiting review, so you do not need to submit it again. Receipt is not approval and does not restore access.

How can I check a previous support request?

Sign in with the account used to submit it. Your requests and any response from Z appear below the support form.

Contact support

Choose the topic that best describes your issue so it reaches the right team. Do not include passwords, full bank or card numbers, or identity documents in your message.
