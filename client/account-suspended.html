Account access

Your account is suspended

You cannot access your account while the suspension is active. Sign in with the suspended account to check an existing appeal or submit a new one. The appeal is reviewed by the Super Admin; submitting it does not automatically restore access.

Verify it is your account

Enter your suspended account's email and password on the sign-in page. Once confirmed, we will check for an existing appeal and let you know if it is still awaiting review.

Sign in to verify and continue

Sign in with the suspended account first. This verifies you own the account, lets us check for an existing request, and prevents duplicate appeals. Do not include your password, full bank or card numbers, or identity documents in the appeal.

  1. Request received
  2. Super Admin review
  3. Decision recorded

Appeal status is available after you sign in with the suspended account. You can also review the account suspension FAQ.

Do not create another account to bypass a suspension. Wait for the account review before trying to sign in again.
